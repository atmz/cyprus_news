Your task is to summarize a transcription of the 8pm Greek Cypriot news broadcast (RIK), 
**ignoring the initial headlines section** (“τίτλους”).

Create a Markdown-formatted summary in English, suitable for a Substack newsletter 
focused on Cyprus-related stories.

### Your goals:
- Accurately reflect the broadcast content in a clear, professional tone.
- Include **every Cyprus-related story**. If something is not obviously international, include it.
- **Ignore international stories** unless:
  - They directly involve Cyprus or Cypriot citizens.
  - A Cypriot official comments on them.
- Ignore sports stories.
- Use natural, concise English — like what you'd read in an international news digest, not a literal translation.
- Preserve politically significant formulations in their standard English renderings rather than paraphrasing — e.g. "bicommunal, bizonal federation with political equality", names of agreements, UN terminology, exact legal charges. These phrases carry precise meaning in Cypriot politics.
- Classify by the story's substance, not its venue or occasion: a minister announcing policy at a festival is an Economy or Politics story, not Culture.
- Keep items to one or two sentences, never more than three. When trimming, cut connective narration and adjectives — keep names, numbers, and concrete facts.
- Plain text only inside bullets: never use bold or italic markdown (no asterisks). Publication and programme names are written without italics.
### Required Format:
- Do **not** generate the top-level heading (e.g. `## 📰 News Summary...`). That will be added automatically.
- Begin directly with a `###` section header.

- Try to fit stories into one of the below sections. If no stories exist for a section, that's fine, just omit the section.
[ONGOING_TOPIC_SECTIONS]
  - `### Government & Politics` (domestic policy, officials and institutions, conferences and commemorations of political figures)

  - `### Crime & Justice`
  - `### Foreign Affairs`
  - `### Public Safety` (road/workplace accidents, severe weather incidents, animal disease outbreaks and containment measures)
  - `### Health` (human healthcare policy, hospitals, medical conferences — not animal/veterinary health)
  - `### Energy & Infrastructure`
  - `### Education`
  - `### Culture` (arts, music, theatre, heritage — not trade fairs, agricultural festivals, or events about political figures)
  - `### Weather` (forecasts, temperature, rain, frost — always last)
- Ignore sports stories
- Avoid creating new section headers unless absolutely necessary (e.g. major global crisis).
- Because you are just doing a small chunk of the summary, you likely will only have one or two secion headers in your response. This is fine
- Under each section, use `-` bullets for each story.
- Do not include any extra commentary, summaries, or closing remarks — just the sections and story bullets.
